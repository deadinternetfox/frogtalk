Everything You Need
A full chat + social + reels + Frog Channel imageboard + music platform, built for privacy.
🔒

End-to-End Encrypted DMs & Rooms

DMs are encrypted client-side with the Signal Protocol (X3DH + Double Ratchet). Private channels use a per-room AES-256-GCM key derived from a shared secret, with AAD-bound ciphertext and automatic key rotation when a member is removed — the server only ever sees ciphertext.

🌐

Frog Social

A full social feed inside the app — post updates with images, tag friends, follow & block, explore trending posts and channels.

🎞️

Reels

Swipe vertical short videos with hot/new/top sorting, fast autoplay, reactions, reposts, and instant comment threads.

📸

Stories

Share 24-hour stories with your friends or a public audience. Filters, privacy controls, and a built-in camera.

🖼️

Frog Channel Imageboard

Anonymous 4chan-style board with threaded replies, post likes, greentext, image/video/audio uploads, live board chat, and moderator approval tools.

🎬

Watch & Listen Together

Dedicated media channel with a persistent "Now Playing" bar. Watch YouTube, queue Spotify & SoundCloud, and listen together in sync.

🔁

Discord & Telegram Bridges

Link a FrogTalk channel to Discord or Telegram. Messages, photos, videos, voice notes and stickers sync both ways in real time.

🧳

Cross-Node Account Sync

Visit another Lilypad and re-sync from home — channel settings, DM prefs, friends, profile themes, client prefs, and FrogSocial posts import with Ed25519-signed exports.

📞

Federated DM Calls

Voice and video calls ring across federation when travelers are connected to your Lilypad — home-server signaling with optional TURN for NAT traversal.

🔞

18+ Channel Labels

Moderators mark public channels with voluntary content categories. Visitors confirm age before history loads — session-scoped, not automated scanning.

📞

Voice & Video Calls

1:1 and group calls over WebRTC P2P. Switch camera, share your screen, or go audio-only mid-call.

🎙️

Voice Messages

Hold to record, release to send. Waveform playback, Telegram-style voice notes that stick around.

🏠

Channels & Directory

Public & private text/voice channels, channel images, pinned messages, roles, and a browsable directory.

👥

Friends & Presence

Add friends, see who's online, set a custom status. Real-time presence and typing indicators across the app.

📎

Rich Media

Images, videos, audio, files, GIFs, link previews, and an image lightbox — all inline, all fast.

😄

Reactions & Replies

React with any emoji and quote-reply to any message to keep threads readable.

💨

Disappearing Messages

Auto-delete DMs after 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. Messages vanish on their own.

🎨

Custom Themes

Pick from Cyberpunk, Ocean, Sakura, Retrowave and more — live preview before you switch.

🔔

Real-time Notifications

Instant WebSocket delivery. Browser & mobile push for DMs, mentions, friend requests and posts you're tagged in.

🖥️

Web, Android & Desktop

Pre-alpha PWA web app, Android APK, Windows portable, and Linux AppImage / .deb. Your account works everywhere — builds are for testing only.

Trust Badges In Network UI

Safe Apply Flow

Tor-Enabled Server Handoffs

The app switches users to the Lilypad's app at /app, preserves handoff tickets when possible, and keeps onion links pointed at the real chat surface instead of the marketing home page.

Built for Privacy,
Not Profit

Most chat apps monetize your conversations. FrogTalk is different — your messages are encrypted before they ever leave your device.

  • ✓ Signal Protocol DMs — X3DH + Double Ratchet, forward secrecy per message
  • ✓ Per-channel AES-256-GCM for private rooms with automatic key rotation on ban/kick — server only sees ciphertext
  • ✓ Verifiable Safety Numbers in the in-app 🔒 panel
  • ✓ WebRTC P2P calls — media never touches our server
  • ✓ Optional 18+ labels on public channels — moderator-set, session age gate (not automated scanning)
  • ✓ No analytics, no third-party SDKs
  • ✓ Self-hosted — run your own instance
  • ✓ SQLite — single file backup, you own your data
